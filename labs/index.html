All Code Labs

Nine small programs that turn the algebra into something you can edit, run and see. Every one starts with working code.

Semester 1

  1. Where Two Lines Cross — Section 03, Systems of Equations and Inequalities. Solve a system, then let two lines check the point.
  2. Move the Line — Section 05, Linear Functions. Change slope and intercept and watch each number do its own job.
  3. Move the Join — Section 06, Piecewise Functions. Move a boundary and make a jump appear or disappear.

Semester 2

  1. The Moment Doubling Wins — Section 01, Exponential Functions. Race repeated addition against repeated multiplication.
  2. Bend the Parabola — Section 02, Quadratic Functions. Move a, b and c while the roots and vertex keep up.
  3. The Quadratic That Eats Itself — Section 03, Solving Quadratic Equations. Repeat z² + c and draw the boundary between escape and capture.
  4. Where the Root Begins — Section 04, Radical Functions. Hit the edge of a square root's real domain in code.
  5. Divide by Almost Zero — Section 05, Rational Expressions. Watch asymptotes emerge from the denominator.
  6. Fit the Mess — Section 06, Data Analysis. Build a least-squares line and inspect every residual.

Start anywhere

These are experiments, not checkpoints. Open one, press Run, change one number, and see whether your prediction survives.
