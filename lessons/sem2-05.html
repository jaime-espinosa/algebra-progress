S2 // SECTION 05 // RATES

Rational Expressions

Two item farms fill one double chest by adding their rates.

The Minecraft structure

A farm rate is items divided by time.

That rate is already a rational expression.

When two item farms fill the same double chest, their rates add.

Their times do not add.

Invert each time to a rate, add the rates, then invert the total.

Adding or averaging the times is the most common mistake.

A farm with zero output takes infinite time to fill the chest.

Time per item is undefined at a rate of zero.

Real farms do not always add cleanly.

A shared hopper line can bottleneck on hopper throughput.

Two mob farms in the same loaded area can steal spawns because of the mob cap.

The combined real rate can therefore be less than the sum.

The algebra

f(x) = P(x) ÷ Q(x)

Q(x) ≠ 0

a/b + c/d = (ad + bc)/(bd)

A rational expression is a ratio of two polynomials.

Factor first, then cancel common factors.

Add fractions only after finding a common denominator.

For f(x) = 1 ÷ x, the domain is all real numbers except 0.

Its range is all real numbers except 0.

Cancelling a factor can remove a hole from the simplified expression.

The original expression is still undefined at that input.

For example, (x − 3)(x + 2) ÷ (x − 3) simplifies to x + 2, but x ≠ 3.

The reciprocal curve

The curve approaches both axes but never touches them.

There is no point at x = 0.

Worked example

Farm A fills a double chest in 12 minutes on its own.

Farm B fills the same double chest in 24 minutes on its own.

Convert each time to a rate.

Farm A rate = 1 chest ÷ 12 minutes

Farm A rate = 1/12 of a chest per minute

Farm B rate = 1 chest ÷ 24 minutes

Farm B rate = 1/24 of a chest per minute

Add the rates over the common denominator 24.

1/12 = 2/24

2/24 + 1/24 = 3/24

3/24 = 1/8 of a chest per minute

Invert the combined rate to get the filling time.

Time = 1 ÷ (1/8)

Time = 8 minutes

Check what each farm delivers in 8 minutes.

Farm A: 8/12 = 2/3 of a chest

Farm B: 8/24 = 1/3 of a chest

2/3 + 1/3 = 1 full chest

The wrong method averages the two times to get (12 + 24) ÷ 2 = 18 minutes.

Eighteen minutes is longer than farm A alone takes, which is obviously impossible.

That contradiction catches the mistake.
