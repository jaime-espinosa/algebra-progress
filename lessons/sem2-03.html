Solving Quadratic Equations

A known area can reveal the side lengths of a farm plot.

The Minecraft structure

A square farm with side length s has area s².

Taking a square root recovers its side length from its area.

A rectangular plot can be four blocks longer than it is wide.

If its width is w, its area is w(w + 4).

Setting that area equal to your tilled-soil count creates a quadratic equation.

A negative root can solve the equation but cannot describe a buildable width.

Block counts are whole numbers, so only whole-number roots give exact buildable dimensions.

Most real block counts produce irrational roots, and rounding a root changes the area.

The algebra

ax² + bx + c = 0, a ≠ 0

x² = k gives x = ±√k when k ≥ 0

x = (−b ± √(b² − 4ac)) ÷ (2a)

Write a quadratic equation in standard form before solving it.

Factoring uses the zero-product property.

The square root property isolates a squared expression.

The quadratic formula works for every quadratic equation.

The discriminant is b² − 4ac.

A positive discriminant gives two distinct real roots.

A zero discriminant gives one repeated real root.

A negative discriminant gives no real roots.

A quadratic has at most two real solutions.

For f(x) = ax² + bx + c, the domain is all real numbers.

If the vertex height is k, the range is [k, ∞) when a > 0.

If the vertex height is k, the range is (−∞, k] when a < 0.

Two real roots

The roots are the x-intercepts.

They sit on opposite sides of x = −0.5, not opposite sides of zero.

Worked example

A plot is four blocks longer than it is wide.

Its total area is 96 blocks.

w(w + 4) = 96

w² + 4w = 96

w² + 4w − 96 = 0

Find two numbers multiplying to −96 and adding to 4.

Those numbers are 12 and −8.

12 · (−8) = −96

12 + (−8) = 4

w² + 12w − 8w − 96 = 0

(w + 12)(w − 8) = 0

w + 12 = 0 or w − 8 = 0

w = −12 or w = 8

Discard w = −12 because a plot cannot have a negative width.

The width is 8 blocks, so the length is 8 + 4 = 12 blocks.

8 × 12 = 96

Now verify the same roots with the quadratic formula.

a = 1, b = 4, c = −96

b² − 4ac = 4² − 4(1)(−96)

b² − 4ac = 16 + 384

b² − 4ac = 400

√400 = 20

w = (−4 ± √400) ÷ (2 · 1)

w = (−4 ± 20) ÷ 2

w = (−4 + 20) ÷ 2 or w = (−4 − 20) ÷ 2

w = 16 ÷ 2 or w = −24 ÷ 2

w = 8 or w = −12

Both methods give the same roots.

Only w = 8 fits the build.
